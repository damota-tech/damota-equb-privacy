Delete your data

Damota Equb · ዳሞታ ዕቁብ

Applies to Damota Equb (members) and Damota Equb Sebsabi (collectors), published by Damota Equb.

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data we hold about you at any time. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

How to ask us to delete your data

Send an email to selamudev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from any email address, and include:

  1. The phone number registered to your account — this is how we find you.
  2. Your name as your collector registered it.
  3. Which app you use: Damota Equb or Damota Equb Sebsabi.

You may also call or send a message to 0904 823 272, or ask your equb collector to make the request for you.

We contact you on that phone number to confirm the request is really yours, then delete the data. This is done within 30 days. We write back to you when it is finished.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-in Deleted. You can no longer sign in.
Your name and phone number Deleted.
Your receipt photos Deleted from our image storage.
Your in-app messages Deleted.
Notification token for your device Deleted.
Payment records of your equb Kept, but with your name and phone number removed, for as long as the equb is running and for 2 years after it ends, so the group can settle a dispute about who paid what. Deleted after that.

If your equb is still running

An equb is a group agreement. If you are still a member of an equb that has not finished, tell your collector before you ask us to delete your data: deleting your account does not end your obligation to the group, and you will lose access to your own payment history.

Other retention

Where the law requires us to keep a record, or where a dispute about a payment is open, we keep only what is needed for that, and delete it once the obligation or the dispute ends.
