Privacy Policy

Damota Equb · ዳሞታ ዕቁብ

Effective date: 25 September 2026

This policy explains how Damota Equb (“we”) handles information in our two apps for equbs (rotating savings groups):

  • Damota Equb — for members of an equb.
  • ዳሞታ ዕቁብ ሰብሳቢ — for the collectors (admins) who run an equb.

Sections up to “Children” describe the members’ app. The collectors’ app has its own section at the end; everything about security, retention and contacting us applies to both.

How accounts are created

You cannot sign up in the app. Your equb collector (admin) registers you when you join an equb, entering your name and phone number. You then sign in with that phone number and a one-time code your collector gives you. If a collector has not registered you, you cannot use the app.

Information we hold

Entered by your collector when registering you

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your ticket number and share in the equb

Created as the equb runs

  • Payment records: which rounds you paid, amounts, dates, payment method, and approval status
  • Round and draw results for your equb

Provided by you in the app

  • Payment receipt photos you choose to attach. The app opens your photo picker only when you tap to add a receipt, and uploads only the photo you select.
  • Messages you send to your equb’s collector in the in-app chat

Stored on your phone

  • A sign-in token, so you stay signed in. It is removed when you sign out.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising. The app shows no ads.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your equb, tickets, rounds, and payments
  • To let your collector record, check, and approve your payments
  • To deliver messages between you and your collector
  • To send you SMS messages from your collector, such as payment confirmations, reminders, and draw results

Who can see it

  • Your equb’s collector(s) can see your name, phone number, payments, receipts, and messages, because they need them to run the equb.
  • Other members cannot see your information. Each member sees only their own tickets, payments, receipts, and messages. Results shared with the whole equb, such as which ticket number won a round, are shown without names or phone numbers.
  • Service providers that host our servers and database process data on our behalf only.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Security

All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is restricted by role: members see only their own records, and collectors see only the equbs they manage.

Retention

We keep your records for as long as you belong to an equb, and afterwards for 2 years so that payment history can be checked if a dispute arises. After that it is deleted.

Your choices

  • Correcting details: ask your collector to update your name or phone number.
  • Deleting your data: email us at selamudev@gmail.com with the phone number registered to your account, or ask your collector. We delete your personal data within 30 days, except payment records we must keep for an open dispute or a legal obligation.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

The collectors’ app — ዳሞታ ዕቁብ ሰብሳቢ

The collectors’ app is for the person who runs an equb. We create the collector’s account; there is no sign-up in that app either. A collector signs in with their phone number and a password, which they can change in the app.

What a collector’s account holds

  • The collector’s name and phone number
  • Their password, stored only as a cryptographic hash — we cannot read it
  • The equbs they manage
  • A notification token for their device, so we can alert them to new payments and messages

Members’ information in the collectors’ app

A collector enters and sees the details of the members of their own equb: names, phone numbers, tickets, payments, receipt photos and messages. A collector can see only the equbs they manage, never another equb. The collector is responsible for entering members’ details accurately and for keeping their phone and password secure.

SMS

The collectors’ app can send SMS messages to members of the collector’s own equb — payment confirmations, reminders and draw results — using the collector’s own phone and SIM, so their mobile operator’s normal charges apply. Messages go only to the phone numbers registered in that equb. The app does not read, receive or store any SMS on the phone, and never sends messages to anyone outside the equb.

Photos and files

  • A collector can view the receipt photos members attach, and attach a photo themselves when they record a payment. The photo picker opens only when they tap to add one.
  • A collector can download their equb’s payment register as an Excel file. Android’s own save dialog decides where the file is stored; the app has no access to the rest of the phone’s storage.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
